## Overview

The [Nobel Prizes digital publication](https://devdatalab.github.io/nobel-prizes-digipub/) presents Development Data Lab's research on access to opportunity among Nobel laureates as an interactive, web-based publication.

## What I Did

I designed and developed the publication, building it as a reusable template for transparent, data-driven research communication across future DDL projects.

## Highlights

- Interactive presentation of DDL research on Nobel laureates
- Built as a reusable template for future DDL publications
