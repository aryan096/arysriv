## Overview

As an undergraduate research assistant with Brown University's Center for Contemporary South Asia and Department of Economics, I built a dataset of geolocated villages and Gram Panchayat election candidates in Uttar Pradesh.

## What I Did

I scraped candidate and election records from the Uttar Pradesh State Election Commission website and matched them to geolocated villages, producing a village-level dataset of local election candidates.

## Outputs

- [_A Dataset of Geolocated Villages and Gram Panchayat Election Candidates in Uttar Pradesh_](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv/d6w2h_v1) (with Aarushi Kalra and Saket Tiwari), a SocArXiv preprint and public dataset with 4,500+ downloads
- Related paper: _Hate at Scale: Elections, Leader Identity and Hate Speech in Rural Uttar Pradesh_ (with Aarushi Kalra), winner of the Brown University Center for Contemporary South Asia Undergraduate Paper Prize
